MARKETS, AI

ANT opens at $111.50, swings to $92.72, and closes its Nasdaq debut up 17% at $97.17

Anthropic's shares opened at $111.50 on the Nasdaq Thursday — roughly 34% above the $83 IPO price set Wednesday night — then traded in a wide band, as high as $115.10 and as low as $92.72, before closing the session at $97.17, up 17.07% from the offer, on more than 846 million shares traded, per CNBC market data as aggregated by financefeeds; this brief did not independently cross-check the print against Nasdaq's own closing tape. [fact; flag, aggregator-sourced] Shares then slipped further after hours, quoted at $92.60, down 4.70% from the close, per Benzinga, with no timestamp given. [fact; flag, aggregator-sourced, timestamp missing] Friday's premarket level could not be confirmed as of this issue's roughly 5:20 a.m. ET press time. [not confirmed as of press time]

Mechanism: that range — a 34% premarket pop, a round trip of more than 20% between session high and low, and a double-digit retreat from the open by the close — is the clearest read yet on how the market is actually pricing Anthropic versus the thesis Wednesday's bankers sold at $83. [read] The $83 offer already sat above the $70–78 range set in the second amended S-1, implying the roughly $1.5 trillion valuation (including options and RSUs) covered in issue No. 41; Thursday's trading shows that even the upsized print undershot where early demand wanted to clear, before giving back a large share of the pop within the same session. [fact/read] A close at $97.17 on 846 million-plus shares is consistent with heavy first-day churn between IPO-allocation holders selling into the pop and new buyers chasing it — the dynamic Renaissance Capital's Matthew Kennedy and University of Florida's Jay Ritter previewed in issue No. 41's framing of $100 as "solid" and $140 as full confirmation of AI enthusiasm; the session's $115.10 high briefly cleared that $100 marker intraday before fading back under it. [read, attribution per issue No. 41]

Second-order consequence: the after-hours slip to $92.60 matters beyond Anthropic's own cap table. The $26 billion private placement already committed by Nvidia, Microsoft, Google, AMD and Broadcom (issue No. 41) ties those companies' own balance sheets to Anthropic's public float for the first time, and Nvidia is still eleven days from Jensen Huang's Dec. 1 House testimony with no reinstatement on the table (see Technology) — so a soft second day for ANT would land on a sector already carrying a leadership-vacuum discount, not a clean one. [read] One trade outlet separately reported that the largest options-tracked short position in ANT was still being added to as of Thursday's pop; this brief could not independently verify that claim and treats it as a single-sourced read, not a count. [flag, single-sourced] The reported 846 million-plus shares traded is itself notable against the roughly 980 million Class A shares Anthropic and selling stockholders put on offer Wednesday (issue No. 41) — rough arithmetic puts first-day turnover north of 85% of the entire float offered, consistent with typical mega-cap-IPO first-session churn rather than a durable buy-and-hold allocation settling in. [read, derived from sourced figures]

Watch: whether ANT's second session holds above Thursday's $97.17 close or slides back toward the $83 offer; whether the move reads as Anthropic-specific or spills into Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom, which are financing part of the deal; and whether index-fund inclusion — flagged in issue No. 41 — begins now that Thursday's volume cleared typical float thresholds for some benchmark methodologies.
